# Design and copy notes

The visual direction is a quiet independent publication: warm ivory, forest green, muted sage, Lora headlines and DM Sans body text. A simple botanical mark, original line drawings and typographic cover concepts give the preview an identity without stock portraits, fake customer stories or heavy image downloads. The supplied brand assets can replace these concepts later.

The composition alternates open editorial sections with one forest-green companion panel and one clear offer. The recognition section stays brief. Numbered steps show the intended buying/access path, while the FAQ keeps detailed limitations available without turning the hero into a legal document. Mobile uses a single column, 17px main body copy, visible focus states and a sticky offer link only after the hero and away from the pricing/footer sections. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.

The implemented headline is **“A little calmer. A little more steady.”** The supporting text names familiar situations and immediately identifies the books and the planned AI companion. It offers a place to begin without promising a compliant child or guaranteed results. “Planned” and “coming soon” labels preserve the distinction between an attractive preview and a purchasable, functioning service.

Three alternatives for later testing, with the same concrete offer and readiness disclosures underneath:

1. **Know what to try in the moments that feel hardest.** Emphasises the parent's next step.
2. **Big feelings. Small steps. A steadier place to start.** Connects the brand to everyday situations.
3. **For the moments you wish came with a guide.** Makes the book-and-follow-up format easy to recognise.

No headline is claimed to convert best. Compare qualified checkout handoffs, verified purchases, successful access, refunds and retention once the actual service works and measurement is appropriately configured. Do not optimise a thank-you view or activation click as though it were a completed purchase or successful activation.
