# Design and copy

The page uses Arial, 18px body copy, dark text on white and a single-column sales flow. Concrete everyday questions replace abstract “AI companion” language and software-style product panels.

The main headline is **“What can I say? What can I cook? What do I try next?”** Supporting copy immediately identifies both ebooks, the AI helper, the €37 monthly price, renewal, cancellation and ebook retention.

The offer uses the requested principles without invented proof: show a desired practical outcome, explain exactly what is included, reduce effort to one guide or one question, demonstrate relevance through example questions, and make the payment/cancellation terms visible. There are no fake testimonials, inflated value stacks, countdowns or unapproved refund guarantees.

The generated front covers show everyday parent-child situations. They use simple readable title typography and plain Little Steady text while the existing logo is unavailable. The text-heavy page remains readable; the artwork is confined to the product covers. Responsive WebPs avoid loading full-resolution PNGs for normal browsing.

The thank-you page is a simple redirect destination: open the guides, find Messenger access in Whop, choose a starter question. It has no payment verification, account or activation backend.

Three headline alternatives for later testing:

1. **Dinner’s refused. Your child’s upset. What do you try next?**
2. **A meal idea. Words to try. Help with your next parenting question.**
3. **For the moments you wish came with a guide.**

No version is claimed to convert best. Measure real purchases, renewals and refunds once the complete service is running.
