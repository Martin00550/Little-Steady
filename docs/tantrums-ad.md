# First tantrum ad

**Headline**

It’s time to leave the park. What do you say when your child starts to melt down?

**Primary text**

Your child wants another turn on the slide. You need to get home. When the tears start, it can be hard to find the words.

Try naming what they want, saying what happens next, and offering help: “You want to keep playing. It’s time to go home. I’ll help you say goodbye to the slide.”

Little Steady shows how to prepare for the ending and what to try if your child is still upset.
